Defects are tiny and easy to miss. Results depend on who is checking, and nothing is recorded.

03 · Our solution

A camera checks every pin and gives PASS or FAIL

Classical vision

Measures the pin’s shape directly. No deep learning, no GPU.

Learns from good pins

Trained on 10–20 good pins. No need to collect every kind of defect.

Low-cost hardware

A Raspberry Pi 5 and a USB webcam, right on the line.

04 · How it works

From camera frame to verdict in six steps

01

Capture

Grab a frame from the webcam

02

Segment

Find the pin’s outline against the paper

03

Align

Rotate it level, head on the left

04

Measure

Length, widths, straightness, symmetry

05

Score

Compare with the good pins

06

Verdict

PASS or FAIL

Edges are measured on a 4× enlarged crop for sub-pixel accuracy, then converted to millimetres (0.155 mm per pixel).

06 · The decision

Two checks, both must pass

1 · Tolerances

Each measurement must be inside its mm limits. A FAIL names the measurement that is out of range.

+

2 · Good-pin model

Mahalanobis distance from the trained good pins. Catches unusual combinations no single limit covers.

=
PASS

only if both hold

Every FAIL explains itself, so operators can trust and act on the result.
